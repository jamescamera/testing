What this score can't tell you

  • —A score of 6 or more doesn't mean you're autistic. A score under 6 doesn't mean you aren't.
  • —The AQ-10 misses people who mask well, and misses more women and people diagnosed later in life.
  • —If you have ADHD, this score is likely to run high whether or not you're also autistic. The two share a lot of surface behaviour.

If you want to take it further

  • 01Book a GP appointment and bring your answers. In England you can ask to be referred under Right to Choose, which is NHS-funded — you pick the provider from a list, but you do need the GP referral first.
  • 02Write down examples before you go: school, friendships, sensory stuff, routines, what you find tiring about other people. Assessments lean heavily on developmental history and it's hard to recall on the spot.
  • 03Assessors usually want an informant who knew you aged four or five — a parent or older sibling. Worth asking them early.
