## Day one — the archive starts here

**What came in**

- One page: delivery system, new pathway, 4 doses a year, 5-minute
  catheterization, silicone tubes, iDRS, plus three sketches of a coil and a
  circle.

**Threads**

Nothing yet — one page cannot connect to anything. This is the baseline, so
here is what to watch for as pages accumulate:

- **Time as the territory.** Both numbers on this page are about time
  (five minutes, four times a year) and both carry marks — a tick and a
  revision. If time keeps showing up on later pages, that is the campaign.
- **The coil.** A drawn shape that recurs across pages is a stronger signal
  than a word that recurs, because you have to decide to draw it again.
- **iDRS.** Underlined and set apart from the claims. Watch whether it ever
  appears inside an idea rather than beside one — that is the test of
  whether it is a real name or internal shorthand.

**Tensions**

None visible yet. The one internal wobble on the page: "Innovation" sits at
the far left as the opening word, but every other fragment is more specific
than it. The page starts with a category and then immediately outgrows it.

**Worth a push**

The five-minute figure was written over another number. That revision is the
most interesting mark on the page — someone changed their mind about how
fast this is. Worth settling: is five minutes fast enough to be the claim on
its own, or does it only land next to whatever it replaces? A procedure time
means nothing without the thing it is shorter than.

And a harder one: if the coil retains itself in place, the patient goes home
with something inside them for three months. Nothing on this page is about
that. The whole page is written from the clinician's side of the room.
